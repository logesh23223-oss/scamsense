Simple Three-Step Workflow

How It Works

Whenever an unexpected message or link creates urgency, pause for 30 seconds and run through these three steps.

Step 01

Enter a suspicious message or website URL

Copy the text message or web link without clicking it and paste it into our Message Checker or URL Checker.

Step 02

Check the warning indicators

Review the highlighted warning signs—such as OTP requests, registration fees, IP-based links, or brand impersonation—and read why each matters.

Step 03

Learn the recommended safety actions

Follow practical next steps to verify independently through official apps, or report fraud immediately via 1930 if money was transferred.

Daily Cyber Hygiene

Five Rules Every Digital User Should Remember

Never share OTPs

Bank staff and support agents never need your one-time password.

Never share UPI PINs

You never enter a PIN or scan a QR code to receive money.

Avoid suspicious links

Skip shortened or urgent links from unknown numbers.

Verify payment requests

Always confirm collect requests and caller identities independently.

Use official websites and apps

Type known bank or .gov.in addresses manually in your browser.

About This Platform

Smart Consumer Protection is an educational and decision-support awareness website designed to help everyday internet users recognise online fraud tactics. It is not a replacement for antivirus software or professional security services.

Privacy First Design

Your pasted messages and URLs are inspected directly inside your own browser using local JavaScript rules. Nothing is stored, logged, or transmitted to any server—and we never ask for OTPs, PINs, or passwords.

Official Reporting Support

If you have experienced financial cyber fraud in India, call the National Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline at 1930 or submit an official complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
