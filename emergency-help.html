Encountered an Online Scam? Take Action Immediately.

If you shared banking details, clicked a suspicious link, or lost money in an online fraud in India, follow these official steps without delay.

Official Indian Cyber Fraud Reporting

Quick reporting gives banks and cybercrime authorities the highest chance of placing a hold on fraudulent money transfers before they are withdrawn.

Report Cyber Crime
Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline
1930
National Toll-Free Helpline (India)
Why quick reporting is important: The first few hours after a financial scam are critical. Reporting immediately via 1930 and your bank helps authorities freeze the fraudster’s receiving account in time. Also beware of fake "recovery agents" online—never pay anyone claiming they can recover stolen money for a fee.

Five Immediate Steps to Take

  1. 1
    Contact your bank or payment service provider immediately.

    Ask them to block compromised cards, freeze netbanking or UPI access, and log an unauthorised transaction dispute.

  2. 2
    Report suspected financial cyber fraud by calling 1930.

    Dial 1930 as soon as possible so the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System can alert the receiving bank.

  3. 3
    Submit a complaint through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

    File your full complaint and upload evidence directly on https://cybercrime.gov.in/.

  4. 4
    Keep transaction IDs, screenshots and other relevant evidence.

    Do not delete suspicious SMS messages, WhatsApp chats, call logs, UPI reference numbers (UTR), or bank statements.

  5. 5
    Avoid sharing OTPs, PINs or passwords with anyone claiming to help.

    Neither bank staff, police, nor 1930 helpline officers will ever ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, or remote screen access.
